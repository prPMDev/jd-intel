jd-intel

Search job posting data from inside your AI. No more copy pasting of job descriptions.

jd-intel gives your AI live access to real openings across seven major hiring platforms. Full text, structured, ready to compare.

Install in one command

  • "What senior PM roles are open right now that I'd be a fit for?" → jd-intel pulls current openings, your AI ranks by your background.
  • "Show me design lead roles open at fintechs." → jd-intel sweeps the sector, your AI filters by what fits.
  • "Find engineering roles at series-B startups that match my background." → jd-intel sweeps the registry, your AI ranks by fit.
  • "Which fintechs are hiring multiple senior engineering roles this week?" → jd-intel scans across companies, your AI surfaces the patterns.
  • "Compare scope across the four staff PM roles I'm looking at." → jd-intel pulls each JD, your AI maps the differences.

Why this exists

Everyone says start with the job boards. But how, exactly? Whichever way you chase an edge, you pay a leverage tax.

Do it yourself with AI

You turn into the data clerk: copy-paste each job description, write a fresh prompt, repeat for the next role. None of the effort compounds.

Lean on the aggregators

They run their own AI and algorithms, ranking you in a black box tuned for their goals. You never see why a role surfaced, or which ones never did.

jd-intel takes the tax off. It hands current, real postings straight to your AI, so your context does the ranking: PM, engineer, or designer roles matched to your background, not a board's. No copy-paste grind, no one else's algorithm deciding what you see.

How it works

  1. You ask your AI for anything that needs current job data.
  2. jd-intel fetches via a small local MCP server.
  3. Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby + 4 more return JDs. Public APIs, not scraping: stable and permitted, where scrapers are fragile and get blocked.

Install

Works with any MCP-aware AI client. Claude Desktop gets a one-click install (below). Other clients use a small config: see the full setup guide. ChatGPT, Gemini, and other non-MCP clients aren't supported yet.

Add to Claude Desktop (no terminal)

No terminal, no Node.js. Claude Desktop runs it for you.

  1. Download the jd-intel extension (a .mcpb file).
  2. In Claude Desktop, open Settings, then Extensions, then Advanced settings, and click Install Extension. Pick the file you downloaded.
  3. Review the access summary, click Install, then start a new chat. The tools appear automatically.

Shortcuts: you can also drag the .mcpb onto the Settings window, or double-click it when your system opens .mcpb files with Claude Desktop. The extension is open source and unsigned, so Claude Desktop shows an "unverified" notice. Choose Install Anyway.
