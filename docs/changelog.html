Changelog

2026-09-28

Added
  • Every response says which board answered and how the company was matched, with the board's own organization name where the platform states it.
  • A rate limit or outage comes back as an error your AI can read, not as "not found".
  • A company that is hiring but has nothing matching your filters now reads as exactly that.
  • detect_ats lists every board a company answers on, registry first.
Changed
  • Requests time out, retry with backoff, honor Retry-After, and run at most four at a time per platform.
  • SmartRecruiters boards read only the postings a call needs: a one-job call on a 400-posting board makes one detail request instead of 400.
  • The response budget has no ceiling; the caller picks it.
  • Tool descriptions describe exactly what the server returns, and a test keeps them that way.
  • Workday location filters use the same word-boundary rule as every other platform.

2026-09-27

Added
  • 218 companies. The registry is now 990, up 28%.
  • Nordic and Dutch coverage grew the most: companies on TeamTailor went from 55 to 90, and on Recruitee from 67 to 97.
  • Healthcare, energy and industrial, and fintech each grew by about a third.
  • Full postings on Lever and Recruitee, including the requirements that were missing before.
  • Pay ranges on Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Lever, Recruitee and Greenhouse, with currency and period.
  • Remote, hybrid or onsite from the platform's own field, and every location a job lists.
  • Responses sized for your AI: complete postings within a token budget, a count of what matched, and paging.
Changed
  • If your AI misspells a search option, it now gets an error naming that option instead of results that quietly ignored it.
  • Errors are now marked as errors, so chat apps can show a failed lookup as a failure.
  • Results also come back as structured data, for chat apps that can display it.
  • All three tools are marked read-only, so chat apps can treat them as safe to run.
  • Netflix moved from Lever to Workday. Marqeta and Fireworks AI moved from Greenhouse to Ashby, and Lumin Digital from Lever to Ashby. All four now resolve to the new platform.
  • Results come newest first.
  • "Posted this week" is accurate on Greenhouse. It used the last-edited date before.
  • Workday boards are read in full instead of stopping at 40 jobs.
  • Descriptions keep their meaning: C++ stays C++ and 8+ years stays 8+ years.
  • Ashby department and team are filled in.
  • Unexpected server errors come back in the same format as every other error.
